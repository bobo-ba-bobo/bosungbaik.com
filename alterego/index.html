Who I Am
Lofi Head Hiphop Dancer Seoul UI / UX Architecture Nerd B-boy Soul Spatial Thinker Est. 1997 Still Moving Design Obsessed

Lofi music is the closest thing I know to organized silence — a beat that holds space without demanding attention.

Hiphop dance is the opposite: pure presence, every move a decision, the body becoming its own interface.

Architecture and UI/UX scratch the same itch — how do you design a space, physical or digital, that makes people feel something before they think anything?
